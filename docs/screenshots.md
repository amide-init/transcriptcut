# Screenshots

A quick look at the app. These are from a synthetic demo clip (a
generated voiceover over a placeholder background) — not real user
content — so nothing here is anyone's actual project.

## Dashboard

No login screen — the dashboard is the entry point. Create, rename, and
delete projects here.

![Project dashboard listing several projects with a New Project button](/screenshots/dashboard.png)

## Editor: video + transcript + timeline

The transcript is the primary editing surface, alongside the video
preview and a zoomable timeline with frame thumbnails and a live
waveform underneath.

![Editor view with video preview, transcript panel, and timeline](/screenshots/editor-overview.png)

## AI tools panel

Explicit, scoped actions — not a free-text command bar. See
[AI-Assisted Editing](/guide/ai-editing) for why.

![AI tools panel with Find filler words and Find long pauses buttons](/screenshots/ai-tools-panel.png)

## Filler-word detection

Detected filler words are underlined in the transcript for review before
you remove them with one click.

![Transcript with detected filler words underlined and a Remove filler words button](/screenshots/filler-words.png)
